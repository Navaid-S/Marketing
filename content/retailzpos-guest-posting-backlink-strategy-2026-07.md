# RetailzPOS — Guest Posting & Backlink Strategy for SEO/AEO/GEO
**Prepared:** July 2026

## 0. Read this before the list — it changes the recommendation

Google's spam policies have hardened specifically around guest posting. The October 2025 spam update explicitly targets **"AI-generated guest post farms" — large-scale operations publishing thin, machine-generated content solely to embed paid backlinks** — as a distinct enforcement category, on top of the long-standing rule that **large-scale guest posting campaigns where the primary purpose is link acquisition (not audience value) count as a link scheme.** John Mueller (Google Search Advocate) has been explicit that guest posting for genuine awareness/credibility/traffic is fine; guest posting purely to manipulate rankings is not. Paid placements are only compliant when tagged `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` — meaning a *paid* guest post link isn't supposed to directly pass SEO ranking value in the first place. [Sources: Blue Tree Digital, seo-kreativ.de, mojolinks.com — Google spam policy analysis, 2026]

**Practical consequence:** almost every site that publishes a public self-serve price list ("$149 for DA 20-30, $299 for DA 50+") is a guest-post-farm-style operation — exactly the category Google is now penalizing harder, and exactly why they publish transparent pricing (it's a commodity link-selling business, not an editorial one). I'm not recommending those. Real trade publications with real editorial standards almost never publish a public "buy a guest post here" rate card — contribution is either free (competitive, unpaid, editorially reviewed) or a negotiated sponsored-content deal you have to contact their sales team about. That's the honest shape of this landscape, and it's why the list below looks different from what a generic search returns.

**One more reframe worth having explicitly:** the retailzpos.com technical SEO audit already on file (`seo-technical-audit-retailzpos-2026-07.md`) found real, foundational issues — a duplicate title tag across four pages, a legacy `/home-old/` still indexed, an unresolved www/non-www split, keyword cannibalization. Those affect "overall website score" more directly and more cheaply than backlinks do. Guest posting is a real lever, but it's the second lever, not the first — fix the foundation, then build authority on top of it.

**Why this still matters for AEO/GEO specifically, even setting pure backlink value aside:** getting RetailzPOS genuinely written about — by name, with real product specifics — on trade publications that AI answer engines already crawl and trust (retail tech press, convenience/liquor trade press) increases the odds RetailzPOS gets *cited* when someone asks ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Mode "what's a good POS system for a liquor store." This is arguably more valuable than the raw link-equity, and it doesn't depend on the link being followed — it depends on the content existing on a source AI models already weight highly. Same logic as the Reddit/Quora AEO doc already built, applied to trade press.

## 1. Tier 1 — Confirmed, real, free editorial programs (start here)

These are the two placements in this research where I could directly confirm the program exists, what it requires, and that it costs nothing but your team's writing time.

### Retail Dive — "Submit An Opinion"
- **URL:** retaildive.com/opinion/submit-opinion
- **What it is:** A real op-ed program at one of the most-cited retail trade publications in the US (part of Industry Dive's publisher network, alongside Supply Chain Dive, Restaurant Dive, Marketing Dive — same submission format at each).
- **Requirements confirmed:** ~1,000 words average; must be original and exclusive to Retail Dive (not published elsewhere, 30-day exclusivity after); **explicitly bans AI-generated submissions** — a human needs to write or substantially rewrite this one; must be a genuine opinion on a timely, important industry issue that adds to an ongoing conversation, not thinly-veiled product promotion.
- **Cost:** Free. Editorial review, not guaranteed acceptance.
- **Angle for RetailzPOS:** a founder/exec op-ed on something like "What independent liquor and convenience retailers actually need from a POS system in 2026" or a compliance-angle piece (age verification, the fake-ID/barcode-scanning stat already used in your social content) — genuine expertise, not a product pitch.

### Retail TouchPoints — "Executive ViewPoints"
- **URL:** retailtouchpoints.com/features/executive-viewpoints
- **What it is:** A bylined contributor program for retail thought leaders — strategy/trend pieces with clear takeaways for retail decision-makers.
- **Requirements confirmed:** Pitch to editors@retailtouchpoints.com first; accepted pieces include author bio, headshot, and a company link.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Angle for RetailzPOS:** the unified-commerce/omnichannel angle already developed in your content strategy work translates directly here.

## 4. What to avoid

Any site whose guest-post offer looks like this is the pattern Google is actively penalizing:
- A public rate card tiered by DA/DR ("$99 for DA20+, $299 for DA50+, $599 for DA70+")
- Accepts submissions across wildly unrelated topics (crypto, casino, CBD, SEO, and retail tech all on the same "write for us" page)
- No real editorial review — payment is the approval process
- Little to no evidence of a real readership (no comments, no social engagement, no author accountability)

These sites carry real penalty risk for the linking site (you), not just the seller, and per the current spam policy language, buying this kind of link is treated as manipulation regardless of whether you or a vendor initiated it.

## 5. Recommended action plan

1. **First:** close the technical SEO gaps already identified in the retailzpos.com audit (duplicate titles, `/home-old/`, www/non-www split) — this moves "website score" more than any single guest post will.
2. **Then:** pitch Retail Dive's Submit An Opinion and Retail TouchPoints' Executive ViewPoints — both free, both real, both already scoped above. Draft two pieces: one compliance/age-verification-angle piece (ties to existing Blog #2 content), one unified-commerce/inventory piece (ties to existing content strategy Pillar 3 ROI material).
3. **In parallel:** send personalized pitches to CSP Daily News, Convenience Store News, and Beverage Dynamics — direct vertical match, real audiences, worth the outreach effort even without confirmed public guidelines.
4. **Skip generic guest-post marketplaces entirely.** If a vendor pitches a "guest post package" with published DA-tiered pricing, treat that as a disqualifying signal, not a value indicator.
5. **Revisit Entrepreneur.com's paid program** once the free/earned placements are underway and there's budget appetite for a broader (not niche-specific) authority play.
