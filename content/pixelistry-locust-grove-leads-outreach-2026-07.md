## 2. Know your competition

Two agencies already market social media/content services specifically into Henry County (McDonough, Stockbridge, Hampton, Locust Grove): **EJM Services** and **Solia Media** (the latter also offers photography, video, and drone). This isn't a reason to avoid the market — it confirms demand exists — but it means your pitch should lean into what a hyper-local, single-operator business can offer that a regional agency can't: personal relationship, faster turnaround, lower minimums, and you physically showing up to shoot content in person.

## 3. Outreach playbook

### Prioritize by fit, not just familiarity
Best-fit leads are businesses where (a) visuals directly drive sales — food, fitness, real estate, pets, home renovation, beauty — and (b) you can already see their social presence is thin or stale. Real estate agents and restaurants are usually the highest-value first targets because they need *recurring* content (new listings, new specials), not a one-time shoot.

### A. In-person / walk-in (highest conversion for a hyper-local business)
- Visit during a slow hour (weekday mid-morning/early afternoon), ask for the owner or manager by name if you have it.
- Bring a one-page "leave-behind" (not a full portfolio pitch) with 2-3 sample photos/reels — ideally shot of *their* storefront or product if you can grab a quick phone shot on the way in, so the pitch is already personalized.
- 30-second pitch: *"Hi, I'm [name] with The Pixelistry — I help small businesses right here in Locust Grove get better photos and videos for social media so you don't have to do it yourself. I'd love to shoot a free short reel for you this week, no strings attached, just to show you what it'd look like. Can I grab your info?"*
- Free-first-piece offer removes risk for them and gives you a real, local portfolio piece to show the next lead.

### B. Chamber of Commerce & networking
- Join the **Locust Grove Area Chamber** (lgchamber.com) — attend a mixer or ribbon-cutting; these events are full of exactly the small-business owners you're targeting, already in a networking mindset.
- Claim/build a free business listing on **Alignable** (alignable.com/locust-grove-ga) — it's a small-business-to-small-business referral network and shows up in local search.
- Chamber ribbon-cuttings for *new* businesses are ideal — a brand-new business has zero existing content and the most urgent need.

### C. Social media (DM/comment) approach
- Follow the business, leave a genuine comment on a recent post (compliment something specific — not "nice page!").
- Then DM: *"Love what you're doing at [business]! I'm a local content creator/photographer here in Locust Grove — I help small businesses like yours with photos and short videos for Instagram/Facebook. Would you be open to a quick call or a free trial shoot?"*
- If their last post is weeks/months old, that's your opening line: *"Noticed it's been a bit since your last post — happy to help take that off your plate."*

### D. Warm referral loop
- After your first 2-3 clients, explicitly ask: *"Do you know another local business owner who could use this?"* Local business owners in a town this size mostly know each other (chamber, rotary, church, kids' school) — referrals will convert far better than cold outreach.
- Offer a small referral incentive (a free add-on post or discount on the next month) to your existing clients for referrals.

### E. Local Facebook groups & Nextdoor
- Groups like "Locust Grove GA Community" or similar town Facebook groups and Nextdoor are where residents already discuss and recommend local businesses — post an intro (once, not spammy) and watch for businesses asking for marketing help or getting mentioned.

### F. Simple starter offer to close the deal
Small businesses rarely have $2,000/month marketing budgets. Structure your first package to be a low-risk yes:
- **Starter:** one on-site shoot/month (photos + 2-3 short-form video clips), $X flat fee, no contract.
- **Growth:** weekly content batches + basic posting/scheduling.
- Lead with the Starter tier and the free first shoot — the goal of visit #1 is a foot in the door, not a signed annual contract.

### Suggested first-week targets
Start with 5-10 walk-ins from the restaurant/cafe, real estate, and home-services categories above (recurring content need + visual product), plus one Chamber event if the timing lines up.
