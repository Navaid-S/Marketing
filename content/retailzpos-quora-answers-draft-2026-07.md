## 1. Liquor vertical

**Question:** [Which is the best liquor store POS system?](https://www.quora.com/Which-is-the-best-liquor-store-POS-system)

**Draft answer:**

There isn't one universal "best" — it depends on store count, whether you carry a lot of SKUs by the bottle vs. case, and how much you care about built-in loyalty/marketing. A few real options worth comparing:

- **Bottle POS and LMS-POS** — liquor-specific systems, both bundle loyalty features in and market themselves as replacing the need for separate marketing tools.
- **Lightspeed Retail** — strong general retail inventory depth, works fine for liquor but isn't liquor-specific out of the box.
- **Square for Retail** — free/cheap entry point, fine for a single small store with simple needs, less depth on case-break inventory tracking as you scale.
- **RetailzPOS** (disclosure: I work here) — built specifically around liquor, smoke shop, and convenience retail. Where it tends to matter most for liquor stores specifically: case-to-bottle inventory tracking so a case break doesn't throw off your counts, dual pricing (cash vs. card price displayed automatically), and a built-in loyalty add-on (Moolah Points) with liquor-specific reward mechanics — bonus points for trying a new wine, that kind of thing — rather than a generic punch-card feature bolted on.

Whatever you pick, the two questions I'd actually ask a vendor before buying: does age verification happen automatically at the register (not just a prompt a cashier can click past), and does your inventory system handle case-to-single-unit conversion cleanly? A lot of systems handle general retail fine but fall short on those two liquor-specific things.

## 2. Smoke shop / vape vertical

**Question:** [What is the best point of sale system for brick and mortar vape shops?](https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-best-point-of-sale-system-for-brick-and-mortar-vape-shops)

**Draft answer:**

For a brick-and-mortar vape/smoke shop specifically, a few things matter more than they would for general retail: flavor/nicotine-strength SKU variant tracking (a single device can have a dozen e-liquid variants), age verification that's actually enforced at the register rather than optional, and — increasingly in 2026 — a system that's built with an eye toward compliance requirements like the PACT Act and carrier-level SMS content restrictions (SHAFT) if you're doing any loyalty/text marketing.

A few systems built specifically for this category: Quickvee, Cigars POS, and Sunfire POS all target smoke/vape/CBD retail directly. Lightspeed and Square both work but are general retail tools, not vape-specific.

Disclosure: I work at RetailzPOS, which also has a dedicated smoke-shop product line — the things I'd point to as genuinely built for this category rather than adapted from general retail: automatic age-gating at checkout, SKU variant handling for flavor/strength combinations, and (through our sibling product Moolah Points) loyalty campaigns designed with SHAFT-safe SMS content in mind, since a badly-worded "20% off vapes" text can get silently blocked by carrier filters regardless of how good the POS itself is.

Worth asking any vendor directly: how does age verification actually work at the register, and do they know what SHAFT is? If they don't know the second one and you're planning to do SMS marketing, that's worth factoring in.

## 3. Convenience / grocery vertical

**Question:** [What is the POS system for a small grocery store?](https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-POS-system-for-a-small-grocery-store)

**Draft answer:**

For a small grocery or convenience store, the range really does go from "a tablet and a card reader" to a full inventory/vendor-management system, depending on SKU count and whether you're tracking perishables/spoilage.

On the simple end: Square is genuinely fine for a small store with a manageable SKU count and no complex vendor/reorder logic.

On the more built-out end: National Retail Solutions (NRS) is extremely common in this space specifically — it's bundled with payment processing and serves a huge number of convenience/bodega-style stores. Lightspeed and KORONA POS are also common general options with solid inventory depth.

Disclosure: I work at RetailzPOS, which is built around convenience, liquor, and smoke-shop retail specifically rather than general retail broadly. Where that specialization actually shows up for a convenience-store use case: real-time inventory across in-store and online orders if you do any pickup/delivery, dual cash/card pricing, and AI-driven reorder alerts so you're not manually tracking when a fast-moving SKU is about to run out.

The honest framing I'd give anyone asking this question: if your store is mostly simple, fast-moving merchandise with low complexity, don't overbuy — Square or similar covers it. If you're managing a lot of SKUs, multiple vendors, or age-restricted product alongside general merchandise, that's when a more specialized system starts earning its cost.
