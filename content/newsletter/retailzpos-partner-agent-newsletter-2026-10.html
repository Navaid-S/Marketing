Your Holiday Season Sales Playbook
Four programs worth raising on your next merchant call — built around the six biggest inventory weeks of the year.
Halloween, Thanksgiving, and New Year's Eve are the three biggest stocking weeks of the year for your liquor, convenience, and smoke/vape merchants — and the best opening you'll get all year to bring real value to the conversation. Here are four RetailzPOS programs and features worth raising this month.
01
Funding

Stocked for the Rush, Not Stretched for It

Every merchant you work with knows the feeling: Thanksgiving week and New Year's Eve mean ordering two to three times the normal case count, right when cash is already tied up in what's already on the shelf. RetailzPOS's cash advance program gives store owners access to working capital against their own sales, so the order gets placed instead of cut short.

Talking pointAsk every merchant this month: what would you stock differently if cash wasn't the limiting factor?
02
New Feature

Invoicely RAI: Your Back-Office Safety Net This Season

More orders means more distributor invoices to check line by line, right when owners have zero spare time. Invoicely RAI reads every supplier invoice automatically and updates inventory and costs in seconds — no manual matching, no late nights after close during the busiest stretch of the year.

Talking pointPair this with the cash advance pitch: we help merchants fund the bigger order, and we remove the paperwork that comes with it.
03
Loyalty

Moolah Points: Turn Holiday Shoppers Into Regulars

A holiday-only customer is only worth one sale. Moolah Points lets merchants run giftable point promotions and compliant SMS/email campaigns — built with SHAFT/TCPA rules in mind — that bring that same customer back during the slow weeks of January, turning one rush into year-round revenue.

Talking pointPosition loyalty as January's insurance policy, not just a holiday nice-to-have.
04
Compliance

Protection on the Busiest, Most Short-Staffed Nights

Costume-covered faces on Halloween, a skeleton crew on New Year's Eve, a rushed cashier mid-Thanksgiving-rush — holiday season is exactly when a skippable ID prompt turns into a real fine or license risk. RetailzPOS's hard-block age verification can't be clicked past, no matter how busy or short-staffed the shift gets.

Talking pointThis is the easiest "why RetailzPOS and not a generic POS" answer you have all season.
