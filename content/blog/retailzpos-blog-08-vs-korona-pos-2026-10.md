**Quick answer:** RetailzPOS and KORONA POS are both inventory-heavy systems built for specialty retail, but they're aimed at different shelves. KORONA POS serves a wide range of specialty categories — liquor, convenience, vape, wineries, gift shops, ticketed venues — with published, tiered pricing starting at $59–$69/month plus add-ons, and a loyalty program built directly into the POS. RetailzPOS stays narrowly focused on liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retail specifically, pairs with Moolah Points (a dedicated loyalty product built around alcohol/tobacco marketing-compliance rules like SHAFT and TCPA), and prices by custom quote rather than publishing tiers. Both vendors describe their age-verification prompts as blocking checkout until a customer is confirmed of legal age, rather than a reminder a cashier can dismiss. If published, predictable pricing matters most to you, that's a real KORONA advantage. If deep, vertical-specific compliance marketing matters more, that's where RetailzPOS is built differently — and it's worth testing both claims with a live demo before deciding.

## RetailzPOS vs. KORONA POS at a glance

**Choose RetailzPOS if** you want a system built exclusively around age-restricted, regulated retail (liquor, smoke/vape, CBD), compliance-aware SMS/email marketing through Moolah Points, AI-driven reorder alerts, and multi-store support designed for that specific category set.

**Choose KORONA POS if** you want published, predictable pricing you can budget against without a sales call, a system that also fits categories outside age-restricted retail (wineries, gift shops, ticketed venues), and a loyalty feature bundled directly into the POS instead of a separate product.

Both are legitimate, inventory-first systems — the real fork is how narrow or broad your retail footprint is, and how much you want pricing transparency versus a custom-fit quote.

## Why this comparison matters

KORONA POS has quietly built one of the more sophisticated content and SEO operations in the specialty-retail POS category — if you've searched for liquor, convenience, or smoke-shop POS software at all, you've likely run into their content. ## Where they actually differ

**Breadth vs. depth.** This is the real difference, more than any single feature. KORONA POS is built to serve specialty retail broadly — a winery, a gift shop, and a liquor store can all run on it. RetailzPOS made the opposite bet: stay narrowly inside liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retail, and go deeper on the specific compliance and marketing problems that category set actually has. Neither approach is wrong — a multi-category specialty retailer may be better served by KORONA's breadth, while a store that's exclusively liquor, smoke/vape, or CBD may get more out of a system built around nothing else.

**Pricing transparency.** KORONA publishes real numbers — Core at $59/month, Retail at $69/month, with clearly listed add-on pricing. RetailzPOS, like most vendors in this specific niche (see our [Bottle POS comparison](/blog/retailzpos-vs-bottle-pos)), prices by custom quote instead. If you want to budget before ever talking to sales, that's a genuine point in KORONA's favor — worth naming plainly rather than glossing over.

**Age verification.** Both vendors describe the same core mechanism: a prompt tied to a restricted SKU that blocks the sale until verification happens, not a dismissible reminder. The real question isn't which vendor claims hard-block enforcement — both do — it's which implementation actually resists a rushed cashier clicking through it on a busy Friday night. Ask both vendors for a live demo of exactly what that screen looks like, not just a feature-sheet claim.

**Loyalty: bundled vs. purpose-built.** The same fork in the road as our Bottle POS comparison, for a similar reason. KORONA's loyalty program lives inside the POS itself — purchase tracking, discounts, and SMS from one system, no separate product to manage. Moolah Points is a dedicated product paired to RetailzPOS, built specifically around alcohol/tobacco marketing-compliance content rules ([SHAFT](https://api.ctia.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/230523-CTIA-Messaging-Principles-and-Best-Practices-FINAL.pdf), [TCPA](https://www.fcc.gov/tags/telephone-consumer-protection-act-tcpa), [DISCUS](https://distilledspirits.org/code-of-responsible-practices/)) rather than general-purpose SMS marketing. If your loyalty marketing needs to navigate what you can and can't legally text a customer about alcohol or tobacco, ask both vendors specifically how their SMS tooling handles that — don't assume a general-purpose loyalty feature already accounts for it.

## Switching from KORONA POS to RetailzPOS: what to expect

- **Inventory import.** Your SKU list, case/bottle configurations, and current stock counts should transfer as part of onboarding — confirm the specific export format KORONA provides and how it maps into RetailzPOS before your switch date.
- **Customer and loyalty data migration.** If you're bringing loyalty balances or customer records over from KORONA's built-in program, ask how that data maps into Moolah Points' reward structure, since the two loyalty models aren't identical.
- **Training.** Staff familiar with KORONA's interface will need time on RetailzPOS's — ask what onboarding support is included and plan a short overlap period rather than a hard cutover.
- **Downtime.** Any POS migration carries some cutover risk. Ask for a specific go-live plan, including a fallback if data doesn't transfer cleanly.

None of this is a reason to avoid switching if RetailzPOS is genuinely the better fit for your category — it's a reason to get a specific migration plan before committing to a date.

## Illustrative example: a multi-category specialty retailer narrowing focus

*The following is a composite, illustrative scenario built from common patterns in specialty retail — not a specific named customer case study.*

Picture a store that started as a general gift-and-specialty shop and has, over a few years, shifted to carry mostly beer, wine, and spirits as that category became the real revenue driver. The POS system handling check-out still treats a bottle of bourbon the same way it treats a candle or a greeting card — inventory works, but nothing about the system is built around the specific problems of selling alcohol: no compliance-aware SMS marketing, no case-to-bottle conversion logic, no audit trail built specifically around age-verification incidents. As the store's product mix keeps narrowing toward liquor, the owner starts wondering whether a broad specialty-retail system is still the right fit, or whether a system built exclusively around what the store has actually become would serve it better.

A category-specific system like RetailzPOS doesn't make that store's inventory "more correct" — it makes the compliance and marketing tooling match what the store actually sells now, instead of what it sold when the POS was first chosen.

## FAQ

**Is KORONA POS or RetailzPOS better for a liquor store specifically?**
Both serve liquor retail. KORONA offers published pricing and also fits stores with a broader category mix (wineries, gift items). RetailzPOS is built exclusively around liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retail, with Moolah Points as a dedicated, compliance-aware loyalty layer.

**Does KORONA POS publish its pricing?**
Yes — KORONA lists a Core plan at $59/month and a Retail plan at $69/month, with add-ons like Food, Plus, and Invoicing ranging from $10–$45/month per terminal. RetailzPOS prices by custom quote instead; confirm current numbers directly with each vendor, since pricing changes.

**Does KORONA POS block a sale if age verification fails?**
KORONA's own documentation describes an automatic prompt tied to restricted SKUs that blocks checkout until verification happens, with both automatic and manual modes available. Ask for a live demo from both vendors to see exactly how the cashier-facing screen behaves under a busy, rushed checkout.

**Can I run SMS marketing campaigns through either platform?**
KORONA includes SMS marketing as part of its bundled loyalty program. RetailzPOS's loyalty layer (Moolah Points) is built with alcohol/tobacco marketing-compliance content rules (SHAFT, TCPA, DISCUS) in mind specifically. Ask both vendors how their SMS tooling handles age-restricted product marketing before assuming either has it fully solved.

**What's involved in switching from KORONA POS to RetailzPOS?**
Inventory import, customer/loyalty data migration, staff training, and a planned go-live cutover. Ask for a specific migration plan — including how loyalty balances transfer — before committing to a switch date.

**Is this comparison biased since RetailzPOS wrote it?**
Fair question — we're transparent that this comes from the RetailzPOS Product Team, and it's written to help you ask sharper questions of both vendors, not to hide KORONA's legitimate strengths, including its pricing transparency. Verify anything that matters to your decision directly with both companies.
