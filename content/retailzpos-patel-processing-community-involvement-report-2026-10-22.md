This is the first of a recurring report, delivered every 4 days, surfacing real community events, sponsorships, and involvement opportunities in Henry County, GA and the surrounding south-metro-Atlanta area where RetailzPOS and Vitreous Alliance / Patel Processing could get involved — as a sponsor, vendor/booth presence, networking participant, or community-giving partner.

# Chamber of Commerce / Networking

**Henry County Chamber of Commerce — ongoing networking programs.** Hosts 20+ events/year, including a monthly "Chamber Connections" meeting and "Business Booster" luncheons, typically the third Thursday of the month. *Get involved:* become a Chamber member, attend luncheons, submit event flyers to the community bulletin board (reviewed within 48 hrs). *Source:* [henrycounty.com/business-networking](https://www.henrycounty.com/business-networking). *Fit:* direct, recurring access to local business owners — the Chamber's own membership is largely small/independent retailers, RetailzPOS's core customer base.

**59th Annual Installation & Leadership Awards Gala (precedent for a 2027 edition).** Henry County Chamber's flagship annual gala; the 2026 edition ran Feb 6, 2026 at Merle Manders Conference Center. No 2027 date posted yet, but worth planning a sponsorship/table around February 2027. *Source:* [business.henrycounty.com — 59th Annual Gala](https://business.henrycounty.com/news/details/henry-county-chamber-of-commerce-honors-2025-leadership-award-winners-at-59th-annual-gala). *Fit:* visibility with the Chamber's full business membership in one room; a sponsor table is a direct brand-awareness play.

**Henry County Chamber Golf Classic.** Annual fundraiser, historically held late Sept/early Oct at Heron Bay Golf & Country Club or Georgia National Golf Club; the 24th annual ran Sept 30, 2024 with sponsorships available. No confirmed 2026 date found — worth a direct call to the Chamber (lrobinson@henrycounty.com / 770-957-5786) to confirm this year's date/sponsorship tiers. *Fit:* classic local B2B sponsorship format, puts the logo in front of area business owners and decision-makers.

# Festivals & Community Events

**Geranium Festival (McDonough).** The city's flagship annual festival, downtown on the McDonough Square; 2026 will be the 49th annual, historically held in May. No exact 2026 date confirmed yet — check geraniumfestival.com directly. Vendor/sponsor booths are part of the festival format. *Fit:* largest single foot-traffic event in the county seat; a vendor booth puts RetailzPOS/Moolah Points in front of the general public and local shop owners who walk the square.

**City of McDonough Christmas Parade & Tree Lighting.** Annual tradition (36+ years running), historically late November on the McDonough Square (2024 ran Nov 30, lighting at 5pm, parade at 6pm). No 2026 date confirmed yet — check City of McDonough / Main Street McDonough. **Timing note: this is likely the single most actionable near-term opportunity in this report, given it falls in the next ~5 weeks.** *Fit:* high-visibility community goodwill event; sponsoring a float or a vendor table reaches local families and merchants alike.

**BridgeFest (Stockbridge).** City of Stockbridge's annual arts & crafts festival, historically late September (2025 ran Sept 27). No 2026 date confirmed. *Source:* [business.henrycounty.com — BridgeFest listing](https://business.henrycounty.com/events/details/bridgfest-23058). *Fit:* second-largest city in the county, similar vendor-booth opportunity — worth tracking for its 2027 date.

**Stockbridge Food Truck Tasty Tuesdays.** Recurring city-run series at Clark Park; confirmed 2026 dates ran June 9–23 and July 14–28. Lower-key but repeatable local visibility if a vendor/info-table presence is allowed — worth confirming with the city for next summer. *Fit:* lighter-touch, repeatable local presence.

*Locust Grove and Hampton turned up no confirmed events in this search — genuinely thin for those two towns specifically; worth checking each city's own site directly.*
