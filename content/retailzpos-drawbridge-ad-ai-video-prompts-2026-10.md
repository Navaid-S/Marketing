# 0. Concept recap

**Inspiration:** the Tesla rocket-powered supercar flip-jump-and-landing stunt, reimagined for RetailzPOS.

**The spot:** a RetailzPOS-branded delivery van reaches a drawbridge mid-rise, launches off the broken span, fires thrusters mid-air to correct its trajectory, lands safely on the far side, and drives on without missing a beat.

**Tagline / VO close:** *"No matter where you are, our team makes sure you get your POS on time."*

**Why it works:** the stunt *is* the metaphor — an obstacle appears without warning, the team adapts in real time, and the delivery still lands exactly where it's supposed to.

This is a tool-agnostic prompt package: the image prompts work in Midjourney, Flux, or DALL·E; the video-motion prompts work in any start/end-frame interpolation model (Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, Kling, Pika).

# 2. Shot-by-shot prompts

**Start Image prompt:**
RetailzPOS delivery van driving along a coastal access road approaching a harbor drawbridge, low golden-hour sun behind it, motion blur on the wheels, driver visible through the windshield, calm and in control, wide low-angle tracking shot. **Style Lock** (see Section 1).

**End Image prompt:**
Same RetailzPOS delivery van now much closer to the drawbridge, warning lights on the bridge control tower just beginning to flash red, bridge gate arm starting to lower in the background, van still at full speed, driver's expression shifting to alert. **Style Lock** (see Section 1).

**Video-generation motion prompt:**
Van accelerates smoothly toward camera along the coastal road, camera does a fast low tracking push following the van's side, wheels kick up light spray/dust, warning lights on the distant bridge tower begin flashing red in the final second, building tension without the van slowing down. Realistic vehicle physics, consistent lighting, 4-second clip.

# 3. Usage notes

- These prompts are tool-agnostic: use any image model (Midjourney, Flux, DALL·E) for the Start/End frames, and any start/end-frame interpolation video model (Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, Kling, Pika) for the motion clips.
- Generate every Start/End image pair with the Style Lock block attached so the van, bridge, and lighting stay visually consistent across all 7 shots, since there's no native memory between independently generated images.
- Expect to run several seed variations per shot and hand-pick the best match to the previous shot's End Image before moving on — continuity across shots is the hardest part of this workflow, not any single image.
- Do not attempt to generate on-screen text, logos, or the tagline directly in an AI image or video model — add those in post-production editing using the background-plate approach in Shot 8.
- Suggested cut order: Shots 1→7 in sequence, Shot 8 as the closing card, total runtime roughly 20–25 seconds before any extended cold open or extra breathing room is added in the edit.
