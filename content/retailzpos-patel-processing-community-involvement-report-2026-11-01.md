# Status updates on previously-reported leads

- **City of McDonough Christmas Parade & Tree Lighting** — still no 2026 date found, even after a dedicated search this edition (including specifically checking for a cancellation — an old headline about a cancelled McDonough parade turned out to be from 2021/COVID, not 2026). **This is now the most-flagged unresolved lead across all four editions.** A direct call to the city remains the only way to close this out.
- **Jingle & Mingle Holiday Market (Junior League of Atlanta)** — the JLA's own page still showed Sunday, Nov 1, 2026 (today) as of this edition's research — no second source ever confirmed it, and the actual 2025 edition was a three-day Nov 13–15 run, so this single-day listing may have been a placeholder. By the time this report is read, it's either already happened or didn't — not confirmable after the fact via search.
- **Locust Grove — Christmas in the Grove** — still no 2026 date; the 2025 pattern (Saturday Nov 29, noon–7pm, City Park) remains the template to expect.
- **Hampton — Christmas Tree Lighting** — still no 2026 date; a new data point strengthens the pattern: 2025 ran Thursday, Nov 27, 7pm, at Terry Jones Depot Park.
- **Stockbridge Holiday Festival & Tree Lighting** — still no 2026 date, and now a complication: two conflicting 2025 listings surfaced — one at the Stockbridge Amphitheater (Dec 5) and a separate "Welcome to Grinchville" event (Dec 20) at VyStar Amphitheater. Unclear whether these are the same event rebranded or two different events — needs direct clarification with the city before relying on either pattern.
- **Marietta Square Merry Market** — still no 2026 date; the city typically announces in late November, so this is worth rechecking next edition.
- **Diwali (Nov 8, now exactly 1 week out)** — still no Henry County event found. New nuance worth flagging: the Atlanta City Hall Diwali tradition has **not** historically run in November — 2024 ran Oct 28, and 2025 ran in October as the "third annual." Given that pattern, **the 2026 edition may have already happened in October and simply been missed by this report's 4-day cadence** — worth a direct check with the Coalition of Hindus of North America (cohna.org) or the Mayor's office on whether it already occurred, rather than continuing to list it as upcoming.
- **Atlanta BeltLine Small Business Saturday Marketplace** — still no 2026 Nov 28 listing found, but a useful pricing benchmark surfaced: a comparable fall 2026 BeltLine Indie Market charged $100 (non-refundable) for a 10×10 retail booth. Also worth knowing: the BeltLine gives **priority placement to vendors who complete its own Small Business Assessment on beltline.org** — worth doing that step now, ahead of the Nov 28 listing going live.

# New opportunities — Metro Atlanta

**Peachtree City — Hometown Holiday, confirmed Saturday, Dec 5, 2026.** Sourced directly from the city's own 2026 sponsorship brochure — one of the few clean, confirmed 2026 dates found across any edition of this report. Format historically includes crafts, food, a gift market, and a tree lighting with Santa arriving at 7pm. *Fit:* a real, dated, sponsorable event in Fayette County, a city not previously covered in this report.

**Kennesaw "Holiday Extravaganza" (light display).** Has published 2026 logistics, per the city's own site: vendor/participant registration deadline **Nov 15, 2026**, installation required by Nov 29, 2026. Distinct from the (still undated) Kennesaw tree-lighting ceremony already flagged. *Fit:* a more process-concrete Kennesaw lead with an actual deadline to act on.

**Arts Clayton Merry Market (Jonesboro, Clayton County).** A real, recurring vendor market, **Saturdays in November and December, 10am–3pm**, 136 South Main Street, Jonesboro. Fills the Jonesboro gap flagged as empty in edition 3. *Fit:* genuinely bookable vendor-market format in a county not otherwise covered, with no single date to wait on — multiple Saturdays to choose from.

**Clayton County Christmas Parade (Jonesboro).** 2025 pattern: Saturday, Dec 6, 3pm, Jonesboro City Center. No 2026 date confirmed yet, but a real, named, recurring event worth tracking next edition.
