# Resolved this edition

**City of McDonough Christmas Parade & Tree Lighting is now confirmed: Saturday, December 5, 2026.** Parade starts 6:00pm in Downtown McDonough. **Limited to the first 80 parade entries, with applications open now.** Tree-lighting time isn't confirmed yet, but the historical pattern (5pm lighting, 6pm parade) suggests the same evening. *Source:* [City of McDonough newsflash/calendar](https://www.mcdonoughga.org/m/newsflash?cat=1). **This closes the single most-flagged unresolved lead across all four prior editions — and because entries are capped at 80, this is a "move now, not later" item if a parade entry is wanted.**

**Atlanta City Hall Diwali — still unresolved, but a live lead surfaced.** No article confirms a 2026 event has happened or has a set date. However, a CoHNA (Coalition of Hindus of North America) post on X reads "Atlanta City Hall is transformed with the spirit of Diwali! Ready to welcome Honorable Mayor Andre Dickens... Shubham Deepavali!" — phrased as a pre-event announcement, not a recap, suggesting an event may be imminent. The exact date couldn't be confirmed from search alone. **With Diwali itself landing Nov 7 this year, if this is genuinely new, the event is likely happening within days.** Worth checking x.com/CoHNAOfficial directly, since that's the one source this research couldn't fully resolve.

# Other status updates

- **Kennesaw "Holiday Extravaganza"** — clarified: this is a **tree-decorating event**, not a lit display with city-provided power (participants get an unlit 6-ft tree + stake and decorate it themselves; lights allowed only if self-powered/solar). $25 individual registration. Nov 15, 2026 registration deadline confirmed — **10 days out.**
- **Stockbridge Holiday Festival** — the earlier Dec 5 vs. Dec 20 conflict partially clarifies: these appear to be **two different 2025 events**, not one rebranded — "Welcome to Grinchville" ran Dec 20 at VyStar Amphitheater, while a separate "Holiday Festival & Tree Lighting" ran Dec 5 at Tracey Wyatt Recreation Complex. No 2026 date for either yet — still needs direct city confirmation.
- **Marietta Square Merry Market** — still no 2026 date; 2025 was the 3rd annual, Dec 6.
- **Smyrna** — still no 2026 date; 2025's Kris Kringle Market ran Dec 2 as part of "Home for the Holidays."
- **Covington "Lighting of the Square"** — still no 2026 date; the pattern is a Thursday the week before Thanksgiving (2024 ran Nov 21), which would put **2026 around Nov 19 if the pattern holds — inference, not confirmed.**
- **Fayetteville "Miracle on Main Street"** — still no 2026 date; pattern is a Saturday in early-to-mid December.
- **Johns Creek Diwali Celebration** — reconfirmed: **Saturday, Nov 7, 2026, 2–7pm, The Boardwalk at Town Center.** Still the only fully confirmed Diwali-adjacent event in the metro area, and now just 2 days out.
- **Thanksgiving-week food drives** — no 2026-confirmed Henry County event found yet. All located precedents are from 2025 or undated; no 2026 sponsor-facing opportunity confirmed.
