# New opportunities — Henry County

**Locust Grove — "Christmas in the Grove."** A new lead filling the gap flagged last edition: a first-ever Light Parade (5pm) followed by a Tree Lighting at City Hall (6pm). The 2025 edition ran Saturday, Nov 29 — no 2026 date confirmed yet. Track directly via chooselocustgrove.com. *Fit:* same community-goodwill logic as the McDonough parade, for a town the first edition couldn't find anything on.

**Hampton — Christmas Tree Lighting (Hampton Depot) & Christmas Parade.** Historically a tree lighting at the Depot plus a parade around early-to-mid December, per Main Street Hampton. The specific year of the sourced listing is unclear — treat as a pattern to verify, not a confirmed date, but it's a genuinely new named lead for the other town the first edition flagged as thin.

**Stockbridge — Holiday Festival & Tree Lighting (Merle Manders Conference Center).** Early December, historically around the first week (one listing: Dec 2, 5–9pm). A second, holiday-specific Stockbridge event distinct from BridgeFest.

**Small Business Saturday (Nov 28, 2026) — Henry County.** No single confirmed sponsorable event, but Choose Henry's standing program routes shoppers to all four Main Street programs (McDonough, Locust Grove, Hampton, Stockbridge) that same weekend every year. Not a bookable sponsorship, but a real, recurring content moment worth a post even without a formal event — e.g. encouraging customers to shop the county's independent retailers that weekend.
