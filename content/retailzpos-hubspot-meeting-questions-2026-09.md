# Context

RetailzPOS currently has no working lead capture, categorization, or lifecycle system in place. Sales Hub Starter is already piloting with the sales team (deal pipeline, contact timeline, click-to-call, meeting scheduler), but Marketing Hub has not yet been configured for lead capture, scoring, or a defined MQL/SQL handoff. This list is organized so Marketing and Sales can each walk in with their own section, with shared and commercial questions at the end.

---

# 1. For Marketing — lead capture & lifecycle setup

1. We currently have zero lead capture set up. Walk us through, end to end, exactly what happens today from a visitor filling out a form to a lead landing in front of someone — on our current Starter tier, not a hypothetical.
2. Do forms and landing pages automatically capture UTM parameters, `gclid`/`fbclid`, and referral source on every submission at our tier, or does that need extra setup?
3. Can we run separate forms/landing pages per vertical (liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, CBD) that route into different lifecycle paths or owners?
4. Can we customize the default lifecycle stages, or add our own? We're mapping our funnel to four stages (unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware) — can that live natively in HubSpot's data model?
5. **Is real lead scoring available at our tier, or does it require Marketing Hub Professional?** If Professional, show us live in the product what we'd actually gain — not a slide.
6. What's HubSpot's own recommended approach to promoting MQL → SQL — is full automation the default, or can we configure it to create a task for a sales rep to review instead of auto-promoting?
7. Can an MQL be flagged for re-engagement review instead of silently reverting to "Lead" if their score decays?
8. Can custom contact properties (persona, vertical, which content they've engaged with) factor into scoring individually, not just one blended number?
9. Does HubSpot support progressive profiling (forms that stop re-asking for info already on file), and is that Starter or Professional?
10. Can gated content (a downloadable report, for example) trigger different follow-up than a direct demo request?
11. How are duplicate or returning contacts handled if someone fills out more than one form over time?

---

# 2. For Marketing — campaigns & attribution

12. We publish across blog/SEO, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit/Quora. What does the campaign tool track natively for organic/earned channels versus paid?
13. **If we start running Google or Meta ads, what's genuinely native at our tier versus what requires the Professional jump** (we were previously quoted $800–890/month) — specifically audience sync back to the ad platforms and conversion-event feedback loops?
14. Does HubSpot connect natively to GA4, or does that need a third-party connector?
15. Can we trace a single closed deal back through every piece of content that touched it — not just the last click — at our current tier, or is that multi-touch attribution gated to Professional too?
16. What does "campaign" mean in HubSpot's model — can one campaign roll up performance across multiple content pieces and channels into one report?

---

# 3. For Sales — handoff & Sales Hub

17. **Sequences, Forecasting, and Playbooks/Reporting are gated to Sales Hub Professional.** What's the actual price difference from Starter, and what's the specific ROI case for a team our size?
18. How does a rep get notified the moment a lead becomes an SQL — email, Slack, mobile push — and is that configurable per rep or per vertical/territory?
19. Can deals route automatically based on vertical (e.g., CBD leads to whoever actually knows that compliance landscape)?
20. Can we customize deal pipeline stages to match our own sales process, with Marketing able to view but not edit it?
21. Does click-to-call and email tracking behave the same on mobile as desktop? Reps are often in-store or on the road, not at a desk.
22. What reporting is actually available to a rep or manager today, at Starter, without Professional's Playbooks & Reporting?

---

# 4. Shared / cross-team

23. What permission controls exist so Marketing and Sales each own their part of the lifecycle without one team accidentally overwriting the other's stage changes?
24. Can we build one shared, live dashboard both teams look at, with demo requests as the headline metric — consistent with how we already measure success internally?
25. Does the tool structurally support a documented "who owns which lifecycle transition" agreement, or is that purely our own internal process to enforce?

---

# 5. Specific to our category — worth asking directly

26. We publish compliance-sensitive content (age-verification, SHAFT/TCPA guidance for smoke/vape retailers, CBD state-by-state rules). Does Marketing Hub's email tooling have any built-in guardrails relevant to regulated-industry marketing, or is that entirely on us to manage?
27. We also have a second, related product (Moolah Points, a consumer-facing loyalty tool for our customers' own shoppers) — is that a HubSpot Marketing Hub use case at all, or fundamentally a different kind of tool? Worth getting a clean, honest answer rather than assuming it fits.

---

# 6. Commercial — the "don't let us get oversold" questions

28. What does onboarding actually look like for a company with zero existing lead capture — guided setup, or do we need an outside partner?
29. What happens if we exceed our current contact tier (1,000 marketing contacts) — a hard stop, a forced upgrade, or an overage fee?
30. Is there a way to trial Professional features before committing annually?
31. **"Show us, live in the product — not a deck — exactly which lifecycle, scoring, and workflow features are Starter versus Professional."**
32. If we start on Starter and outgrow it in three months, what does upgrading actually involve — data migration, downtime, re-setup work?
33. What's included in the $3,000 Professional onboarding fee specifically, and is it negotiable given we're already a paying customer?
34. Can you point us to a comparable customer — regulated, multi-vertical, B2B retail software — rather than a generic case study?
35. Realistic timeline from "we sign today" to leads actually flowing through a working lifecycle system?
