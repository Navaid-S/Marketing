# Moolah Points — 4 Static Image Social Posts
**Source:** Built directly from `moolahpoints-content-strategy-2026-07.md` — one static-image post per core content pillar (Pillar 2 objection-handling, Pillar 3 ROI proof, Pillar 1 compliance authority, Pillar 4 SMS-first education). Pillar 5 (reviews/benchmark report) is better suited to real customer photos than a generated graphic, so it's left out of this batch.

**A note on the AI image prompts:** AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, etc.) render layout, icons, color, and composition well, but render *exact small text* unreliably. Each prompt below is written to generate the visual design/layout/iconography — the specific numbers, headers, and labels should be added afterward in a design tool (Canva, Photoshop, Figma) for crisp, accurate text. I've flagged this per-post rather than leaving it as a surprise.

**Brand assumption:** No official Moolah Points brand color palette exists in this repo yet, so the prompts below use a placeholder palette (warm gold/amber + deep charcoal-navy — a "money/rewards" association fitting the name). Swap in real brand hex codes once they exist.

## Post 1 — "Free Loyalty Isn't Free" (Pillar 2: Objection-Handling)

**Platform:** Instagram/Facebook feed (also works on LinkedIn)
**Format:** Static comparison graphic, 1:1 square

**Caption:**
"Free loyalty isn't free — it's just unpaid for by you and unbuilt for your store. Clover Rewards is free. Loyverse is free. But 'free' usually means generic points and nothing built specifically for liquor, vape, or convenience retail. Here's what that leaves out."

**CTA:** "See what a loyalty program built for liquor, vape, and c-store actually includes — link in bio."

**Hashtags:** #IndependentRetail #LiquorStoreOwner #SmokeShopBusiness #CStoreLife #SmallBusinessLoyalty #RetailTech

**AI image prompt:**
> A modern, clean flat-design marketing graphic for a comparison chart, split into two vertical columns on a soft off-white background. Left column header area labeled in muted gray tones, right column header area in bold deep-amber/gold gradient. Each column has three rows with simple minimalist line icons (a punch-card icon, a smartphone icon, a padlock/lock icon) with empty space beside each for a short label. Right column icons are colorful (gold, deep teal) and filled-style; left column icons are gray, outlined, and slightly faded, visually communicating "basic vs. built for you." Overall style: modern fintech app aesthetic, rounded corners, soft drop shadows, generous white space, geometric sans-serif typography feel (like Poppins or Inter), a small coin or money-themed logomark motif subtly worked into one corner. Color palette: warm gold/amber (#D4A017-ish), deep charcoal-navy (#1A2238-ish), off-white background. Square 1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution, professional B2B SaaS marketing style, vector-illustration quality, no photographic elements, no readable text rendered by the model.

**Add in post-production:** Column headers ("Generic Free Loyalty" / "Moolah Points") and the three row labels (e.g., "Generic points-only" vs. "Liquor/vape/c-store reward mechanics"; "Card or app required" vs. "Phone number only"; "Locked to one POS" vs. "Data portable").

## Post 2 — "Your 4th-Visit Customer" (Pillar 3: ROI Proof)

**Platform:** Instagram/Facebook + LinkedIn
**Format:** Static stat-card graphic, 1:1 or 4:5 vertical

**Caption:**
"Your 4th-visit customer is worth more than you think. Return rate jumps to ~95% after visit #4 — most stores lose that customer before visit #2 ever happens. Loyalty members in convenience retail also spend about 12% more per visit than non-members (Paytronix, 2026). The math is real. Here's why the first month with a new customer matters most."

**CTA:** "Turn a first-time buyer into a regular — see how Moolah tracks it automatically. Link in bio."

**Hashtags:** #ConvenienceStore #LiquorStoreMarketing #RetailROI #SmallBusinessGrowth #LoyaltyProgram

**AI image prompt:**
> A bold, high-contrast statistics infographic-style social media graphic, styled like a premium fintech dashboard card. Center composition features two large circular progress-ring (donut chart) graphics side by side, connected by a subtle upward-curving arrow suggesting growth over time. The left ring is mostly empty/unfilled in a light muted tone; the right ring is almost fully filled in a rich gold/amber gradient with a soft glow highlight. Small empty label space beneath each ring. Background is a deep charcoal-navy gradient with faint abstract geometric line patterns (subtle receipt or barcode texture) barely visible. A small minimalist shopping-bag or receipt icon tucked in one corner. Clean generous negative space, premium SaaS-analytics-dashboard aesthetic, vector/flat-illustration style with subtle gradients, no photographic people, no readable text rendered by the model. Square 1:1 or vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, high resolution.

**Add in post-production:** "<50%" under the left ring, "95%" under the right ring, small captions ("Return rate after visit 1" / "Return rate after visit 4+"), and optionally the 12% check-lift stat as a smaller supporting callout.

## Post 3 — "What SHAFT Compliance Means for Your Loyalty Texts" (Pillar 1: Compliance Authority)

**Platform:** Instagram/Facebook (works as a carousel cover slide too) + LinkedIn
**Format:** Static educational/explainer graphic, 1:1 square

**Caption:**
"Your loyalty texts can get your number blocked by carrier filters if they're not written right. Here's what SHAFT compliance actually means for a liquor or vape shop's SMS campaigns — and how to avoid it. Save this for later."

**CTA:** "Download our free SMS compliance checklist for regulated retail — link in bio."

**Hashtags:** #RetailCompliance #SmokeShopBusiness #LiquorStoreOwner #SMSMarketing #TCPA

**AI image prompt:**
> An editorial-style explainer infographic for social media. Central focal element: a stylized smartphone mockup displaying a text-message bubble UI, with a small shield-and-checkmark badge icon overlaid near the top of the phone to convey "protected/compliant." Arranged around the phone in a loose arc, five small circular icon badges, each containing a distinct minimalist line icon (a flame, a heart, a wine glass with a diagonal slash, a firearm silhouette with a diagonal slash, a cigarette with a diagonal slash) with empty space above each badge reserved for a single bold letter. Color palette: deep charcoal-navy background, warm amber/gold accent color for the shield and badge outlines, soft white and light-gray supporting elements. Style: clean vector infographic, flat design with subtle drop shadows, professional trust-and-safety / compliance-tech aesthetic, generous breathing room, no photographic elements, no readable text or letters rendered by the model. Square 1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution.

**Add in post-production:** The letters S-H-A-F-T above their respective icon badges, and a small caption line under each (Sex, Hate, Alcohol, Firearms, Tobacco) — precision matters here since it's compliance-education content, so don't skip proofing this text.

## Post 4 — "72% More Likely to Join via Text" (Pillar 4: SMS-First Education)

**Platform:** Instagram/Facebook + X
**Format:** Static single-stat graphic, 1:1 square

**Caption:**
"72% of people are more likely to join a loyalty program if you text them the invite instead of asking them to download an app. Liquor runs, vape refills, and gas-station coffee are impulse buys — nobody's downloading an app for that. That's why Moolah Points just needs a phone number."

**CTA:** "No app. No card. Just a phone number. Link in bio."

**Hashtags:** #SMSMarketing #LoyaltyProgram #RetailTech #SmallBusinessMarketing #CStoreLife

**AI image prompt:**
> A bold, single-focus statistic graphic for social media, dominated by a large empty numeral space taking up roughly half the frame, styled as an extra-bold modern sans-serif number rendered in a rich gold/amber gradient with a subtle glossy highlight (leave the digit shape generic/placeholder since exact numerals should be added in post). Beside or beneath the number area, a small minimalist line-art illustration contrasting two icons: a smartphone with a text-message speech-bubble icon on one side, and an app-store/download icon with a diagonal slash through it on the other, visually contrasting "text" vs. "app download." Background is a deep charcoal-navy solid or subtle gradient, with a faint scattered pattern of small retail-themed icons (shopping bag, receipt, coin) at low opacity in the background for texture without clutter. Clean generous white space, premium modern fintech/SaaS marketing aesthetic, vector flat-illustration style, no photographic elements, no readable text rendered by the model. Square 1:1 aspect ratio, high resolution.

**Add in post-production:** The "72%" numeral in the reserved space, plus a short supporting label ("more likely to join via text invite").
