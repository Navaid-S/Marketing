# 0. What this covers, and a note on the data

This document has three parts: (1) comparable job postings/titles found across hiring sites for a role that blends content writing, social media, and light video production, (2) a salary range for Henry County, GA and the surrounding Atlanta metro, and (3) a ready-to-post job description for the role.

**Synthesis:** for a single hire covering content writing, social media management, and light video/reels production — entry-to-mid level, Henry County/south Atlanta metro — a reasonable base salary range is:

### **$45,000 – $58,000/year**, with room up to **~$62,000** for a candidate who's genuinely strong across all three (writing, social strategy, *and* video editing), since that combination commands a premium over any single skill alone.

If the role leans more junior/entry on video (willing to train that piece) and stronger on writing/social, the lower half of the range ($45K–$52K) is more appropriate. If you want someone who walks in already shooting and editing reels well, budget toward $55K–$62K.

# 3. Job posting — ready to use

*Copy the block below directly into Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, or Henry County-area job boards. Placeholders in brackets need your input before posting.*

---

**Job Title:** Content & Social Media Creator — RetailzPOS & Patel Processing

**Location:** Henry County, GA (McDonough area) — [confirm: on-site / hybrid / remote-eligible]
**Employment Type:** Full-time
**Level:** Entry to Mid-Level
**Compensation:** $45,000–$62,000/year, based on experience [confirm before posting]

**About the Role**

We're looking for a content-savvy, social-first creator to help tell the story of two growing brands: **RetailzPOS**, a point-of-sale platform built for liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retailers, and **Patel Processing**, our credit card processing company currently undergoing a full brand refresh. This is a hands-on role for someone who can write it, plan it, post it, and — when needed — pick up a camera and shoot it.

You'll work across both brands, helping RetailzPOS stay visible and relevant in a fast-moving retail-tech space, while helping build Patel Processing's new look and voice from the ground up.

**What You'll Do**

- Write blog posts, social captions, and marketing copy for RetailzPOS and Patel Processing in each brand's own voice
- Plan and maintain a content calendar across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube)
- Research daily/weekly trending topics, formats, and sounds, and adapt them into real, brand-relevant content ideas — not generic reposts
- Shoot and edit short-form video and Reels for both brands, including light on-location filming (store visits, team content, brand reveal content for Patel Processing's rebrand)
- Support Patel Processing's brand relaunch with consistent visual and written content across the transition
- Track what's actually performing and adjust the content plan accordingly
- Collaborate with the broader marketing effort already underway for both brands

**What We're Looking For**

- 0–3 years of experience in content writing, social media, or a related marketing role (entry-level candidates with a strong portfolio are welcome to apply)
- Genuinely social-media fluent — you already know what's trending and why, not just how to schedule a post
- Comfortable writing for different audiences and tones (consumer-facing retail content vs. B2B payments/fintech content)
- Basic-to-intermediate video shooting and editing skills (phone-shot content is fine; experience with CapCut, Premiere Pro, Final Cut, or similar is a plus)
- Strong writing fundamentals — clear, specific, no fluff
- Organized enough to manage a content calendar across two brands without things slipping

**Nice to Have**

- Experience in retail, fintech/payments, or B2B SaaS marketing
- Basic graphic design skills (Canva or similar)
- A portfolio or reel samples you can share

**To Apply:** [insert application instructions — email, application link, or portal]
