# 0. What this covers, and a note on the data

This document has three parts: (1) comparable job postings/titles found across hiring sites for a role that blends content planning, writing, social media, and on-set/on-camera support, (2) a salary range for Henry County, GA and the surrounding Atlanta metro, and (3) a ready-to-post job description for the role.

**Scope clarification:** this is not a video-producer hire. The candidate plans content, writes, researches trends, assists with logistics on shoots (setup, prompts, coordination), and appears on camera as a talking head when needed — but does **not** operate the camera independently or edit footage. That distinction changes both the comparable-role match in Section 1 and the salary synthesis in Section 2 from the earlier draft of this document.

**Honesty about the data:** No job board (Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor) could be queried directly from this environment — everything below comes from search-engine results, aggregator sites, and salary-research platforms (Payscale, Built In, Plane, Robert Half), cross-referenced where possible. **No current job postings specific to Henry County or McDonough, GA matched this role** — local employer demand for "digital marketing / content / social media" skills is mentioned generically in a few places, but nothing names a specific local opening. Everything in the salary range is built from **Atlanta-metro proxies**, which is the best available substitute for a market only ~30 miles southeast of the city, not a McDonough-specific figure. Where a source looked templated or unreliable, it's flagged as such rather than treated as real data.

# 1. Comparable roles found on hiring sites

With shooting and editing off the table, the closest real-world match shifts away from "content creator" (which almost always implies hands-on camera/editing work in actual postings) and toward **content strategist / social media coordinator** titles, with on-camera presence as a named but secondary duty. The postings found cluster into a few patterns:

| Title pattern | Example posting | Level | What it actually covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Social Media Coordinator / Specialist** | Payscale/Built In benchmark roles, Atlanta | Entry/Mid | Content calendar, captions, trend research, performance tracking — the core of this role minus production |
| **Entry Level Social Media Agent** | Fresh Form Draft (Atlanta, GA) | Entry/Junior (1-3 yrs) | $21.50–$31.50/hr; modest requirements (HS diploma or equivalent); no production duties |
| **Marketing Content / Social Media Specialist** | Mediabistro (x2 listings) | Mid | Writing + social strategy + light visual-content support, not solo production |
| **Content Writer, fintech/payments** | Optimized Payments (hybrid, Atlanta-based, mostly remote) | Mid | Blog posts, social media, white papers, case studies, SEO, for a payments-industry audience — the closest match to the **Patel Processing** side of this role specifically |
| **Web Content Specialist** | Henry County Government (McDonough, GA) | Entry/Mid | The one genuinely local posting found — writing, editing, and managing content across departments; no salary listed |
| **On-camera talent / host (secondary duty)** | Not a standalone title in postings found | — | Appearing on camera as a talking head almost never shows up as its own job at this level — it's typically folded into a marketing/social role as one listed responsibility, exactly as it should be framed here |

**The pattern worth noting:** once shooting and editing are removed, this role is best framed as a **content strategist/coordinator who is also comfortable on camera** — not a "creator" title, which in real postings almost always signals hands-on production work. That's a meaningfully different (and generally lower-paid) role than the one in the earlier draft of this document.

**Synthesis:** for a hire covering content planning/strategy, writing, social media management, trend research, on-set assistance, and on-camera appearances (no independent shooting or editing) — entry-to-mid level, Henry County/south Atlanta metro — a reasonable base salary range is:

### **$42,000 – $55,000/year**, with room up to **~$58,000** for a candidate with real experience planning content that demonstrably drove views/engagement growth, since that strategic skill is worth more than general social-media fluency alone.

This is **lower than the earlier draft's $45K–$62K range**, specifically because shooting and editing — the skills that justified the top end before — are no longer part of the job. On-camera comfort is a real asset worth paying for, but it doesn't command the same premium as production skills do in the market data above. If the role leans more junior (willing to train someone up on trend research and content strategy), the lower half ($42K–$48K) fits. If you want someone who already has a demonstrated track record of planning content that performed, budget toward $52K–$58K.

# 3. Job posting — ready to use

**Job Title:** Content Strategist & Social Media Coordinator — RetailzPOS & Patel Processing

**Location:** Henry County, GA (McDonough area) — [confirm: on-site / hybrid / remote-eligible]
**Employment Type:** Full-time
**Level:** Entry to Mid-Level
**Compensation:** $42,000–$58,000/year, based on experience [confirm before posting]

**About the Role**

We're looking for a content-savvy, social-first planner to help tell the story of two growing brands: **RetailzPOS**, a point-of-sale platform built for liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retailers, and **Patel Processing**, our credit card processing company currently undergoing a full brand refresh. This role is about the thinking and the writing as much as the output — you'll figure out what content should actually get made to drive views and engagement, write it, help produce it, and be a face in front of the camera when a piece calls for it.

You won't be running the camera or editing footage yourself — our team handles that. You'll be the person making sure what gets shot is actually worth shooting.

**What You'll Do**

- Research trending topics, formats, and sounds daily/weekly, and turn them into a real content plan built to actually grow views and engagement — not generic reposts
- Write blog posts, social captions, video scripts, and marketing copy for RetailzPOS and Patel Processing in each brand's own voice
- Plan and maintain a content calendar across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube)
- Assist with video shoots — logistics, setup, prompting, coordinating locations and schedules — working alongside whoever's on camera and editing
- Appear on camera as a talking head or presenter when a piece of content calls for it
- Support Patel Processing's brand relaunch with consistent written and planned content across the transition
- Track what's actually performing and adjust the content plan accordingly
- Collaborate with the broader marketing effort already underway for both brands

**What We're Looking For**

- 0–3 years of experience in content strategy, social media, marketing, or a related field (entry-level candidates with a strong portfolio or demonstrated social-media instincts are welcome to apply)
- Genuinely social-media fluent — you already know what's trending and why, and can turn that into a real content plan, not just a list of ideas
- Comfortable writing for different audiences and tones (consumer-facing retail content vs. B2B payments/fintech content)
- Comfortable being on camera — confident, natural on-screen presence
- Strong writing fundamentals — clear, specific, no fluff
- Organized enough to manage a content calendar and shoot logistics across two brands without things slipping

**Nice to Have**

- Experience in retail, fintech/payments, or B2B SaaS marketing
- A portfolio, reel samples, or examples of content plans that drove real engagement growth
- Basic graphic design skills (Canva or similar)
- Prior on-camera or presenting experience
