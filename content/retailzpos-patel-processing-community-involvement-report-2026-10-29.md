# New opportunities — Metro Atlanta

**Atlanta BeltLine / Atlanta Indie Market — Small Business Saturday Marketplace.** A real, recurring, vendor-bookable event (the 2025 edition ran at BeltLine trail locations, pop-up tents alongside the Marketplace shipping containers). Booth fees ran roughly **$43–$103** for a 10×10 tent (vendor supplies own tables/chairs/display). Organized by Atlanta Indie Market + Atlanta BeltLine Inc., who also run **monthly vendor pop-ups at four BeltLine locations year-round** — not just the one Saturday. No 2026 vendor-application link found yet — watch Eventeny and Atlanta Indie Market's own channels. *Fit:* the first genuinely bookable Small Business Saturday opportunity found in this report (Henry County's version is routing/visibility only, not a vendor slot) — strong foot-traffic visibility in Atlanta proper, and the monthly pop-up option is a lower-commitment way to test it before Nov 28.

**Conyers Christmas Parade (Rockdale County) — confirmed for 2026.** **Saturday, Dec 5, 2026, 2–3pm**, Olde Town Pavilion, Olde Town Conyers. A new county for this report. No route or vendor/sponsor info found yet — check the City of Conyers or Olde Town Conyers page directly. *Fit:* a clean, dated, confirmed 2026 event — rare in this report so far — worth a quick inquiry into parade sponsorship or a vendor presence nearby.

**Duluth — Deck the Hall / Handmade Holiday Market (Gwinnett County).** Confirmed **Saturday, Dec 5, 2026, roughly 2–7pm**. Organized by the Atlanta Foundation for Public Spaces. The main vendor-application deadline was **Oct 26, 2026 — already passed**, but a separate food-truck/vendor deadline (**Nov 21, 2026**) may still be open. *Fit:* closes the Gwinnett/Duluth gap flagged as untested in edition 2. Worth emailing events@duluthga.net about late/waitlist spots or the food-vendor track, and flagging the earlier application deadline for 2027 planning.
