AISLE 02

Color System

5 TOKENS + STATUS

Built from the real PtechPOS mark — register teal stays fixed from the logo. Rather than pair it with its true complement (a cold red near 12°), the second accent sits at roughly 130° — an analogous produce green, not a contrast color — because this brand lives next to the produce aisle and the bread rack, not a warning light.

Paper White
#F7F4EC · Primary ground
Warm, papery, never sterile-grey — this is a grocery counter, not a hospital scanner.
Register Ink
#1C1A16 · Primary text
Warm near-black, matched to the wordmark's "PTECH."
POS Teal
#14758A · Primary brand accent
Straight from the logo's "POS." The one color every asset should be recognizable by.
Produce Green
#3F8F52 · Analogous accent
Teal's neighbor, not its foil. Produce, freshness, fresh-case energy — reserve for callouts, never body text.
Paper Stone
#8A8172 · Secondary neutral
Rules, borders, captions — warm-biased, matched to the paper ground, never a cool gray.
Status: Approved / Declined
#1E88C7 · #C23B3B · Functional only
Register-state colors for checkout mockups — not brand accents. Never used decoratively.
WHITE 60% INK 27% TEAL 8% 5%

A working 60/27/8/5 ratio per asset — ground, ink, one confident accent, one sparing accent. Status green/red are never part of this ratio; they only appear inside checkout-state mockups.

Teal (192°) and Produce Green (~130°) sit as an analogous pair rather than a contrast pair, deliberately calmer than teal's cold true complement — closer to a fresh-produce case than to an alarm. This is the only sanctioned two-color pairing for hero moments; Paper Stone carries everything structural.

AISLE 07

Do / Don't

HOUSE RULES

Do

  • Lead with supermarket and bodega/convenience content — that's the actual business.
  • Mention liquor-store fit briefly when relevant; don't build a campaign around it.
  • Keep headlines in Big Shoulders Display, all caps, tight leading.
  • Use JetBrains Mono only for data, SKUs, and small print — never headlines or long body copy.

Don't

  • Don't lead marketing with liquor retail — that's RetailzPOS's lane, not this one.
  • Don't set body copy in Produce Green on Paper White — contrast fails accessibility checks.
  • Don't use status blue/red as decorative brand color — they're register states only.
  • Don't recolor, stretch, or merge the "PTECH" / "POS" split.
