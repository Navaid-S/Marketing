AISLE 01

Foundation

MISSION & VOICE

AISLE 02

Color System

5 TOKENS + STATUS

Built from the real PtechPOS mark — ink black and register teal. Teal sits near 192° on the hue wheel; rather than jump to its true complement (a cold red near 12°), the accent lands warmer and more citrus — closer to shelf-tag orange than warning red — because this brand lives next to produce aisles and price tags, not alarms.

WHITE 60% INK 27% TEAL 8% 5%

A working 60/27/8/5 ratio per asset — ground, ink, one confident accent, one sparing accent. Status green/red are never part of this ratio; they only appear inside checkout-state mockups.

Teal (192°) and Shelf Orange (~25°) sit as a warm/cool near-complementary pair, deliberately warmer than teal's cold true complement — closer to citrus and shelf tags than to an alarm. This is the only sanctioned high-contrast pairing for hero moments; Barcode Slate carries everything structural.

AISLE 07

Do / Don't

HOUSE RULES

Do

  • Lead with supermarket and bodega/convenience content — that's the actual business.
  • Mention liquor-store fit briefly when relevant; don't build a campaign around it.
  • Keep headlines in Chakra Petch, all caps, tight leading.
  • Use IBM Plex Mono only for data, SKUs, and small print — never headlines or long body copy.

Don't

  • Don't lead marketing with liquor retail — that's RetailzPOS's lane, not this one.
  • Don't set body copy in Shelf Orange on White — contrast fails accessibility checks.
  • Don't use status green/red as decorative brand color — they're register states only.
  • Don't recolor, stretch, or merge the "PTECH" / "POS" split.
