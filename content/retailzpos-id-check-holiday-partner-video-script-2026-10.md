# "Protect Your Merchants During the Holiday Rush" — Partner Video Script

**Format:** Talking-head, Partner Liaison direct to camera
**Length:** ~75–90 seconds (script below runs ~160 words spoken)
**Audience:** RetailzPOS partners/agents (share via partner portal, email, LinkedIn, or partner newsletter)
**Goal:** Give partners the ID Check pitch in the Partner Liaison's own words, so they walk into merchant conversations with it already internalized — not reading off a page
**Source:** Adapted from the "Protect Your Merchants During the Holiday Rush" partner post

---

## Shot notes

- Vertical or square framing, good light, simple background — a desk, an office, or even a store floor works
- Direct to camera throughout, conversational energy, not a read-aloud
- On-screen text overlays suggested at the bracketed cues below, matching the four icons from the original post (🪪 👥 ⚡ 🛡️) so the video and the written post feel like one campaign
- No B-roll required — this works as a single continuous take, but a quick insert of a checkout/ID-scan moment at the [B-ROLL OPTIONAL] cue is a nice-to-have, not a requirement

---

## Script

**[COLD OPEN — on camera]**

Hey, it's [PARTNER LIAISON NAME] from RetailzPOS. Quick one before the holiday rush really hits.

**[Beat]**

You already know what the next few weeks look like for your merchants — more customers, longer lines, staff stretched thin. What doesn't slow down during all that? Age verification. If anything, it gets harder, because a rushed cashier on a packed Saturday night is exactly the moment a quick glance replaces an actual check.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT: 🪪 Age verification stays part of checkout]**

That's the first thing I want you walking into merchant conversations with: RetailzPOS ID Check keeps age verification *part of the checkout process itself* — not a separate step a busy employee can skip past when the line's out the door.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT: 👥 One consistent process, every employee]**

Second — it's not about any one cashier remembering the rules. It helps every employee on shift follow the *same* consistent process, whether it's their first week or their fiftieth shift during a holiday rush.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT: ⚡ Built for exactly this kind of pressure — B-ROLL OPTIONAL: quick checkout/ID-scan insert]**

Third — this is built for exactly the moment your merchants are worried about: when holiday traffic puts the most pressure on staff, that's precisely when they need the most support, not less.

**[ON-SCREEN TEXT: 🛡️ Protects the business]**

And at the end of the day, it's about protecting the business — from underage sales, from the fines and compliance headaches that come with a missed check, during the exact weeks when the stakes and the traffic are both highest.

**[Beat — direct to camera]**

So if you've got a merchant worried about the holiday rush — and compliance alongside it — this is a conversation worth having now, not after the first busy weekend. Reach out if you want help framing it for a specific merchant. Talk soon.

**[END CARD: RetailzPOS ID Check — questions? Contact your Partner Liaison]**

---

## Notes for the Partner Liaison

- Swap in your own name and delivery style freely — this is a script to work from, not to recite word-for-word. The goal is it sounds like you, not a script.
- If you'd rather open with a direct stat or a specific merchant scenario instead of "quick one before the holiday rush," that works too — the structure (problem → three benefit beats → protect-the-business close → CTA) is what matters, not the exact wording.
- Runtime flexes naturally between 70–100 seconds depending on pacing — no need to hit an exact mark.
