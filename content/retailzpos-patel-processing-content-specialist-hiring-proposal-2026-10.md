# Why now

Our content output has grown past what I can keep producing solo. Between the daily trend research, the blog library, the AEO/compliance content tied to our holiday push, and the social calendar, we're already running a real content operation for RetailzPOS. On top of that, Patel Processing's brand relaunch needs its own steady stream of content starting now, not once the new look is finalized. I need someone who can take real ownership of the planning and writing side, help me get more out of our shoots, and be another face in front of the camera when a piece calls for it — without me needing to hand off something as specialized as production or editing, which we're keeping in-house as-is.

# What I found: comparable roles in the market

I looked across the major hiring sites and a number of salary-research platforms to see how companies are actually titling and structuring this kind of role. A few honest caveats up front: I couldn't query Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, or Glassdoor directly from here, so everything below comes from search results and aggregator/salary sites, cross-checked where I could. And **I found no job postings specific to Henry County or McDonough** for this type of role — the one genuinely local listing was a Henry County Government Web Content Specialist posting, which didn't list a salary. Everything else is an Atlanta-metro comparison, which is the best proxy available for a market 30 miles southeast of the city.

Here's what the real postings look like once I accounted for the fact that our hire won't be shooting or editing independently — just planning, writing, assisting on shoots, and appearing on camera:

| Title pattern | Example | Level | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social Media Coordinator / Specialist | Payscale/Built In benchmarks, Atlanta | Entry/Mid | Content calendar, captions, trend research, performance tracking — the core of what I'm proposing |
| Entry Level Social Media Agent | Fresh Form Draft, Atlanta | Entry/Junior | $21.50–$31.50/hr; minimal requirements |
| Marketing Content / Social Media Specialist | Mediabistro (two listings) | Mid | Writing + social strategy + light visual-content support |
| Content Writer (fintech/payments) | Optimized Payments, Atlanta (hybrid) | Mid | Blog, social, case studies, SEO, for a payments audience — the closest match I found for the Patel Processing side specifically |
| Web Content Specialist | Henry County Government, McDonough | Entry/Mid | The only genuinely local posting I found; no salary listed |

**What I took away from this:** once production is off the table, "Content Creator" isn't the right title — in real postings, that title almost always implies hands-on shooting and editing. "Content Specialist" is the cleaner, more commonly searched title that still accurately covers what this role actually does.

# What I found: compensation

Same caveat as above — no Henry County–specific pay data exists that I could find, so I'm working from Atlanta-metro benchmarks for the closest comparable titles. Henry County's cost of living runs a bit below core Atlanta, so actual local pay likely lands at or slightly below these figures.

| Role proxy | Range | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Social Media Coordinator, Atlanta | $41,000–$55,000 (avg ~$48,000) | Payscale |
| Content Specialist, Atlanta | $57,000–$67,000 (avg $63,383) | Payscale — likely skews toward a more senior, writing-only version of this role |
| Entry-level Social Media Agent, Atlanta | ~$45,000–$65,500 (annualized from $21.50–$31.50/hr) | Live posting, Fresh Form Draft |
| Remote Content Marketing Specialist, Georgia (statewide) | ~$46,359 median | Plane — remote/base-only, likely understates on-site pay |
| Digital Content Specialist, Atlanta (public sector) | $50,000–$60,000 entry | GA Dept. of Economic Development posting |

**My recommendation: $42,000–$58,000/year base.** I landed here specifically because this version of the role doesn't include shooting or editing — those are the skills that would normally justify the top end of a "content creator" range, and we're keeping that work in-house. On-camera comfort is a real, valuable asset, but it doesn't command the same premium in the market as production skills do. I'd lean toward the lower half ($42K–$48K) if we're open to training someone up on the trend-research and planning side, and toward the top ($52K–$58K) for a candidate who already has a track record of planning content that actually moved the needle on views.

**About the Role**

We're looking for a content-savvy, social-first planner to help tell the story of two growing brands: **RetailzPOS**, a point-of-sale platform built for liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retailers, and **Patel Processing**, our credit card processing company currently undergoing a full brand refresh. This role is about the thinking and the writing as much as the output — you'll figure out what content should actually get made to drive views and engagement, write it, help produce it, and be a face in front of the camera when a piece calls for it.

You won't be running the camera or editing footage yourself — our team handles that. You'll be the person making sure what gets shot is actually worth shooting.

**What You'll Do**

- Research trending topics, formats, and sounds daily/weekly, and turn them into a real content plan built to actually grow views and engagement — not generic reposts
- Write blog posts, social captions, video scripts, and marketing copy for RetailzPOS and Patel Processing in each brand's own voice
- Plan and maintain a content calendar across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube)
- Assist with video shoots — logistics, setup, prompting, coordinating locations and schedules — working alongside whoever's on camera and editing
- Appear on camera as a talking head or presenter when a piece of content calls for it
- Support Patel Processing's brand relaunch with consistent written and planned content across the transition
- Track what's actually performing and adjust the content plan accordingly
- Collaborate with the broader marketing effort already underway for both brands

**What We're Looking For**

- 0–3 years of experience in content strategy, social media, marketing, or a related field (entry-level candidates with a strong portfolio or demonstrated social-media instincts are welcome to apply)
- Genuinely social-media fluent — you already know what's trending and why, and can turn that into a real content plan, not just a list of ideas
- Comfortable writing for different audiences and tones (consumer-facing retail content vs. B2B payments/fintech content)
- Comfortable being on camera — confident, natural on-screen presence
- Strong writing fundamentals — clear, specific, no fluff
- Organized enough to manage a content calendar and shoot logistics across two brands without things slipping

**Nice to Have**

- Experience in retail, fintech/payments, or B2B SaaS marketing
- A portfolio, reel samples, or examples of content plans that drove real engagement growth
- Basic graphic design skills (Canva or similar)
- Prior on-camera or presenting experience
