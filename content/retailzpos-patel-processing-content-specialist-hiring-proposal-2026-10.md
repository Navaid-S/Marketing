Our content output has grown past what I can keep producing solo. Between the daily trend research, the blog library, the AEO/compliance content tied to our holiday push, and the social calendar, we're already running a real content operation for RetailzPOS — and it's working: we're actively tracking and improving how often AI search tools cite us. On top of that, Patel Processing's brand relaunch needs its own steady stream of content starting now, not once the new look is finalized. I need someone who can take real ownership of research, strategy, and writing — including the search/AI-citation side specifically — help me get more out of our shoots, and be another face in front of the camera when a piece calls for it, without me needing to hand off production or editing, which we're keeping in-house as-is.

# What I found: comparable roles in the market

I looked across the major hiring sites and a number of salary-research platforms to see how companies are actually titling and structuring this kind of role. A few honest caveats up front: I couldn't query Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, or Glassdoor directly from here, so everything below comes from search results and aggregator/salary sites, cross-checked where I could. And **I found no job postings specific to Henry County or McDonough** for this type of role — the one genuinely local listing was a Henry County Government Web Content Specialist posting, which didn't list a salary. Everything else is an Atlanta-metro comparison, which is the best proxy available for a market 30 miles southeast of the city.

Once I added the SEO/AEO/GEO requirement, the closest real-world matches split into two families — content/social roles and SEO-specialist roles, with AEO/GEO showing up as a named skill line inside SEO and content-writer postings rather than its own title yet:

| Title pattern | Example | Level | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social Media Coordinator / Specialist | Payscale/Built In benchmarks, Atlanta | Entry/Mid | Content calendar, captions, trend research, performance tracking |
| SEO Specialist | Mondo, Atlanta (live posting, hybrid) | Mid | $70,000–$80,000; the clearest pay comp for the search-strategy half of this role |
| Content Writer — GEO/AEO | HumanWiz (via iimjobs) | Mid/Senior | Confirms AEO/GEO is now a named, hireable skill, not a niche ask |
| AI Content Specialist | Insight Partners portfolio role | Entry/Mid | Explicitly for "strong B2B writers who understand SEO fundamentals and are developing hands-on AEO/GEO expertise" — the closest level-match to what I'm proposing |
| Content Writer (fintech/payments) | Optimized Payments, Atlanta (hybrid) | Mid | Blog, social, case studies, SEO, for a payments audience — the closest match I found for the Patel Processing side specifically |
| Web Content Specialist | Henry County Government, McDonough | Entry/Mid | The only genuinely local posting I found; no salary listed |

**What I took away from this:** "Content Creator" still isn't the right title once production is off the table, and a pure "SEO Specialist" title undersells the social/content side of what we need. **"Content Specialist & Social Media Coordinator"** is the title that actually matches the blend — and the market increasingly expects someone in this kind of role to understand AEO/GEO as part of the job, not as a specialist add-on.

**Budget I'm working within: $42,000–$55,000/year base.** Worth flagging honestly: the market data above puts AEO/SEO strategy skill closer to $60K–$80K in Atlanta, so this range is competitive for the content-writing and social-coordination side of the role but sits below where dedicated SEO/AEO experience actually prices. Practically, that likely means we're hiring someone with strong research and writing fundamentals and real interest in AEO/GEO who builds that expertise with us, rather than someone who walks in with it already proven. I think that's a reasonable trade-off at this budget — just wanted to be upfront that it's a trade-off, not a market-rate match for the full scope.

**About the Role**

We're looking for a content-savvy, research-driven writer to help tell the story of two growing brands: **RetailzPOS**, a point-of-sale platform built for liquor, convenience, smoke/vape, and CBD retailers, and **Patel Processing**, our credit card processing company currently undergoing a full brand refresh. This role sits at the intersection of great writing, social strategy, and modern search — we need content that doesn't just read well, it gets found and cited. That means writing for traditional SEO and for how AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews) actually select and cite sources, alongside planning and writing the social content that drives views and engagement day to day.

You'll also help with video shoots and appear on camera as a talking head when a piece calls for it — but you won't be running the camera or editing footage yourself; our team handles that.

**What You'll Do**

- Write blog posts, comparison/best-for/alternatives pages, FAQ content, social captions, video scripts, and marketing copy for RetailzPOS and Patel Processing in each brand's own voice
- Research and apply AEO/GEO (Answer Engine/Generative Engine Optimization) strategy — structuring content so AI search tools actually cite it, not just traditional keyword SEO
- Research daily/weekly trending topics and competitor content, and turn both into a real content plan built to grow views, engagement, and search/AI visibility — not generic reposts
- Plan and maintain a content calendar across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube) and across our written content library
- Track what's actually ranking, getting cited, and performing — and adjust the content plan based on real results, not guesswork
- Assist with video shoots — logistics, setup, prompting, coordinating locations and schedules — working alongside whoever's on camera and editing
- Appear on camera as a talking head or presenter when a piece of content calls for it
- Support Patel Processing's brand relaunch with consistent written and planned content across the transition
- Collaborate with the broader marketing effort already underway for both brands

**What We're Looking For**

- **Great research and writing skills, above all else** — you can research a topic properly and write it clearly, specifically, and without fluff
- Working knowledge of SEO fundamentals, plus real interest in or experience with AEO/GEO — how AI search tools decide what to cite, and how to write content that earns that citation
- 0–3 years of experience in content writing, SEO, social media, or a related field (entry-level candidates with a strong writing portfolio are welcome to apply)
- Genuinely social-media fluent — you already know what's trending and why, and can turn that into a real content plan
- Comfortable writing for different audiences and tones (consumer-facing retail content vs. B2B payments/fintech content)
- Comfortable being on camera — confident, natural on-screen presence
- Organized enough to manage a content calendar and shoot logistics across two brands without things slipping

**Nice to Have**

- Hands-on experience with AEO/GEO content strategy — structuring content for AI citation, FAQPage/structured data basics, or researching which sources AI engines cite in your industry
- Experience in retail, fintech/payments, or B2B SaaS content/SEO
- A portfolio of writing samples or content plans that drove measurable traffic, engagement, or citation results
- Basic graphic design skills (Canva or similar)
- Prior on-camera or presenting experience
